How to use this 3D view
  1. Watch the water flow. Click As the water flows to follow the blue drop through the system. Use Pause to take a closer look, Resume to continue, or End tour to return to the overview. Selecting equipment or moving the model ends the tour so you can explore freely.
  2. Explore equipment. Click an equipment label or a visible component. Use Previous and Next below the model to follow the water path. Its description and photographs appear in the information panel.
  3. Rotate and zoom. Press and hold on an empty part of the model, then drag. Use Zoom in and Zoom out, or scroll with your mouse. On a touchscreen, drag with one finger and use the zoom buttons.
  4. Return to a clear view. Use Overview for the whole area, Overhead to look down, or a named view to explore the tanks, discharge or ventilation shaft. Click Overview if you lose your place.
  5. View photographs. Click View photo or an additional photograph to enlarge it. Click Close to return.
  6. Explore another source. Click Back to site map at the top of the page.
