Photo-informed, not scanned. Colors and surface character are visual approximations. Every material includes its source photo and reuse notes. The package makes no claim about measured dimensions, structural condition or hidden construction.

What’s in the package

12 material sets. Each includes a 512 × 512 base-color, normal and roughness map, plus a physical tiling scale in feet. The manifest records the source evidence, intended uses and finish settings.

Apply with context

Use the material factory for the web model or import the maps into Blender. Confirm the finish against photos of each new water source. These surfaces do not transfer Apple Hill’s asset IDs or system assumptions to another property.
