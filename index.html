How to use the site map
  1. Choose a water source. Click West Garage, AH-2 or AH-3 above the map, or click a dark marker.
  2. Open the model. In the information panel, click Explore water source to enter the interactive 3D view.
  3. Find supporting equipment. Click a red marker, or use the equipment search below the map.
  4. Move around the map. Drag to move, use + and − to zoom, and click Show full site to return to the whole property. On a touchscreen, drag with one finger and pinch to zoom.
